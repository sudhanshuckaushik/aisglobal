Finland had 2,325 Indian full degree students in 2024, the sixth largest international group, according to the Finnish National Agency for Education. Graduates can apply for a residence permit of up to two years to look for work.

The community

Official Vipunen data published by the Finnish National Agency for Education show 2,325 Indian nationals studying for full degrees in Finland in 2024, 0.7% of all students. India ranked sixth among international student nationalities, behind China, Bangladesh, Nepal, Sri Lanka and Pakistan. 843 Indians started degree studies in 2024. After graduating, international students may apply for a residence permit to look for work for up to two years.
