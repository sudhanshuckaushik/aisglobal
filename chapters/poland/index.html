Poland had 3,180 students from India in 2024/25, according to Statistics Poland, making India the sixth-largest foreign nationality at Polish universities.

The community

Statistics Poland counted 108,609 foreign students in 2024/25, including 3,180 citizens of India, behind Ukraine, Belarus, Turkey, Zimbabwe and Azerbaijan. 615 Indian students were in their first year, and 666 graduated that year. Graduates of Polish universities can apply once for a 9-month temporary residence permit to look for work or start a business.
