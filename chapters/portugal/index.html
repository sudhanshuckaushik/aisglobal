Portugal is a small destination for Indian students, with about 400 internationally mobile tertiary students from India in 2024, though the number has roughly doubled since 2019.

The community

Eurostat counted 403 mobile tertiary students from India in Portugal in 2024, up from 189 in 2019. Most international students in Portugal come from Portuguese-speaking countries, led by Brazil.

The number of mobile tertiary students from India in Portugal more than doubled between 2019 (189) and 2024 (403), according to Eurostat. Source
