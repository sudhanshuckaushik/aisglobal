The Netherlands had 3,679 Indian degree students in 2025-26, making India the third-largest source country outside the European Economic Area. Graduates can apply for a one-year orientation year residence permit to look for work.

The community

Nuffic counted 3,679 Indian degree students at Dutch universities and universities of applied sciences in 2025-26, up from 3,553 the year before. Indian students are concentrated at master's level: new master's enrolments from India rose 16.5% in 2025-26. The orientation year permit, valid for one year, is open to graduates of Dutch programmes and of top 200 ranked foreign universities.

New master's enrolments from India at Dutch institutions rose by 16.5% in 2025-26, even as total international enrolment fell slightly. Source
