Hungary had 1,279 students from India in 2024, according to the Hungarian Central Statistical Office, placing India ninth among source countries.

The community

The number of Indian students in Hungary rose from 1,127 in 2023 to 1,279 in 2024, based on KSH data. The Embassy of India in Budapest estimates about 1,500 Indian students across universities in Budapest, Pécs, Szeged and Debrecen. Under the India-Hungary Education Exchange Programme, up to 200 fully funded Stipendium Hungaricum scholarships are offered to Indian students each year.
