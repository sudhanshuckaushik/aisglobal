Malaysia offers degrees from foreign branch campuses, including the University of Nottingham and Monash University, alongside its own public and private universities.

The community

India's Ministry of External Affairs counted 4,412 Indian students in Malaysian universities and tertiary institutions as of January 2025. Malaysia's 2020 census counted about 2.0 million people of Indian ethnicity (6.7 per cent). Graduates can apply for a 12-month Graduate Pass, which is open to Indian nationals until 31 December 2026.

Graduates of the University of Nottingham Malaysia receive the same University of Nottingham degree certificate as students who study in the UK. Source
