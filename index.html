    01 · Our mandate

    One body, speaking for Indian students in every country they study.

    AIS is the international umbrella for Indian student communities abroad. Chapters work on the ground; AIS carries their case to the people who decide.

    Policy advocacy

    Visa rules, post-study work rights, immigration reform and student protections, argued directly with lawmakers and regulators.

    In the pressCNN, October 2025: NAAIS on how US visa policy is pushing Indian students to look elsewhere, describing "a climate of fear and uncertainty". Read

    Government relations

    Formal working relationships with education ministries, host governments and Indian missions abroad.

    In the pressMay 2021: NAAIS shipped its first 40 oxygen concentrators to India in partnership with the Embassy of India and the American Association of Physicians of Indian Origin. The News Minute

    Student support

    Visa guidance, pro bono legal aid, mental health support, housing help, scholarships and career connections.

    In the press2021: NAAIS ran workshops across the US on immigration, visas, OPT and CPT, internships and healthcare. The PIE News

    Community in a crisis

    A standing network means no student abroad faces an emergency alone, whether it's on campus or back home.

    In the pressJune 2020: with lockdowns closing campuses, NAAIS estimated 75,000 Indian students might be stuck in the US and helped students with groceries and housing. The PIE News

    04 · Why it matters

    Indian students are now the largest international group in the United States and in Germany. The rules they live under are written by people who have never met them.

    1.25M

    Indian students in higher education abroad as of 1 January 2025 (12,54,013), according to India's Ministry of External Affairs. Counting school students, the total is 18,82,318.

    India MEA data, via ICEF Monitor, Dec 2025
    363,019

    Indian students in the United States in 2024/25. India is the leading place of origin, ahead of China at 265,919.

    IIE Open Doors 2025
    59,000

    Just under 59,000 Indian students in Germany in winter semester 2024/25, up 20% in a year and the largest international group, ahead of China at about 38,600.

    DAAD, Nov 2025
    −5.7%

    The number of Indian students in higher education abroad fell in 2025. Canada cut new study permits and US F-1 visa issuances to Indians fell 44% in the first half of FY2025. When the rules change, students need someone in the room.

    ICEF Monitor, Dec 2025

    06 · The issues

    What we work on, and why it matters now.

    The questions Indian students bring to us most, with the evidence behind each.

    1. 01

      Study abroad, done ethically

      Honest information before a student commits: what a degree really costs, what the job market really looks like, and which agents and institutions to avoid. No student should arrive abroad on a promise that was never true.

    2. 02

      Support while they study, and help settling in

      A chapter on the ground for housing, mental health, legal questions and emergencies, and for the everyday work of settling in: finding community, understanding local law and workplace norms, and becoming part of the place they now live.

    3. 03

      Visas and study permits

      Whether a student can come at all. US F-1 visa issuances to Indian students fell 44% in the first half of FY2025, and Canada issued 9,955 new study permits to Indians from January to August 2025, against 149,875 in the same months of 2023. ICEF Monitor

    4. 04

      Work rights during and after study

      OPT and CPT in the US, and post-study work visas elsewhere. Under the India-Australia ECTA, Indian graduates can stay up to 2 years after a bachelor's, 3 after a master's and 4 after a doctorate. DFAT
