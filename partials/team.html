Chair of the Board

Divy Trivedi

Divy Trivedi is a business leader and social entrepreneur from Gandhinagar, Gujarat. He is Managing Director of Infinite Play & Entertainment and a Director of Vakradanta Management Consultants, and holds leadership roles in two chambers of commerce. His community work has focused on youth, women's welfare and wellbeing.

Roles

  • Vice President, The South Indian Chamber of Commerce & Industry
  • National Vice President, South Asian Chamber of Commerce & Industry
  • Managing Director, Infinite Play & Entertainment Pvt. Ltd.
  • Director, Vakradanta Management Consultants Pvt. Ltd.

Community work

  • Organised a World Yoga Day programme with the Ahmedabad District Administration in which 750 children with disabilities performed silent yoga, recorded as a world record
  • Worked with district officials to help 5,229 women receive widow pension benefits paid directly to their bank accounts
  • Organised a city cleanliness programme with 300 sanitation workers

Recognition

  • State Youth Award 2019, Government of Gujarat
  • National Outstanding Youth Award
  • National Gaurav Award, International Youth Society

Education

  • B.Com and LLB
  • Postgraduate diploma in journalism and mass communication
